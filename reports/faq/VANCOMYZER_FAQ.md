## What is AUC-guided vancomycin dosing?
AUC-guided vancomycin dosing focuses on estimated drug exposure over time rather than relying only on trough levels. The goal is to support exposure-based interpretation in a more clinically meaningful way.

## How is Bayesian dosing different from traditional PK dosing?
Bayesian dosing uses prior model information together with patient-specific data to support individualized interpretation. Traditional PK approaches can still be useful, but the two methods differ in how they estimate exposure and how they handle sparse information.

## Why does transparency matter in a vancomycin dosing tool?
Transparency matters because clinicians need to understand what inputs, assumptions, and methods influenced a recommendation. Visible reasoning supports trust, review, and safer interpretation.

## What should clinicians review before accepting a recommendation?
Clinicians should review the major inputs used, the method applied, assumptions affecting the result, uncertainty or limitation notes, and any safety-related cautions.

## Why might AUC interpretation differ from trough-based reasoning?
AUC interpretation focuses on total exposure rather than using trough concentration alone as a proxy. Because of that, the clinical interpretation and resulting dosing decisions may differ.

## Can documentation-ready summaries improve workflow?
Documentation-ready summaries can support communication, review, and consistency by making recommendations, assumptions, and cautions easier to copy, share, and understand.

## What kinds of cases are useful for learning vancomycin dosing?
