## Purpose
Define how Vancomyzer should turn educational and intelligence assets into short-form content for audience growth, trust-building, and repeat visibility.

## Primary goals
- repurpose educational assets efficiently
- reinforce clinician trust and interpretability
- maintain useful, non-hype communication
- connect short-form content to deeper resources

## Recommended content categories

### 1. Clinical insight posts
- short practical notes on AUC, Bayesian dosing, workflow, or caution points
- useful even without clicking through

### 2. Mini explainers
- short explanations of common concepts
- Bayesian vs traditional PK
- trough vs AUC
- why transparency matters

### 3. Case spotlights
- one teaching point from a realistic case
- connect to sample case pages or documentation examples

### 4. Product/workflow snippets
- short explanations of transparency features
- explain workflow value without sounding overpromotional

### 5. FAQ snippets
- short answers to common clinician questions
- link back to FAQ or resource hub

## Platform guidance

### LinkedIn
- longer educational posts
- practical, thoughtful, clinician-oriented tone
- good for explainers and case spotlights

### X / short-form posts
- concise and high-signal
- focus on one insight per post
- useful for quick explainers and FAQ snippets

- avoid unsupported claims
- avoid hype-heavy promotion
- keep each post educational first
- align product mentions with actual functionality
- prefer clarity over cleverness
