# Vancomyzer Contact / Institutional Page Implementation Spec — Refined

## Purpose
Define a build-ready Contact / Institutional page that helps serious evaluators understand what Vancomyzer supports, what they should review first, and how to proceed without aggressive sales framing.

## Refined page objective
A clinical evaluator visiting this page should be able to:
1. understand whether Vancomyzer is relevant to their workflow
2. see what materials they should review first
3. understand the trust- and documentation-oriented strengths of the product
4. choose between a soft demo-first path and direct contact

## Final page structure

### Section 1 — Page intro
#### Required elements
- page headline
- short evaluator-focused summary
- primary CTA
- optional secondary CTA

#### Content requirements
- clarify who the page is for
- keep tone concrete and professional
- avoid generic enterprise language
- avoid sales-heavy positioning

---

### Section 2 — Who this is for
#### Required audience groups
- antimicrobial stewardship teams
- ICU and ID pharmacists
- clinicians involved in vancomycin dosing review
- clinical leaders evaluating workflow support tools

#### Content requirements
- define intended evaluators clearly
- avoid overbroad audience language
- align page with actual high-fit users

---

### Section 3 — Why teams may care
#### Required themes
- workflow clarity
- interpretability of outputs
- visible assumptions and limitations
- documentation-ready summaries

#### Content requirements
- explain practical team value
- stay evidence-aware
- avoid unsupported ROI, productivity, or outcome claims

---

### Section 4 — What evaluators should review first
#### Required elements
- one sample case
- one workflow explanation path
- one documentation/export example
- one trust/evidence reference

#### Content requirements
- support a demo-first evaluation path
- reduce friction before contact
- make next steps obvious

---

### Section 5 — Contact / evaluation CTA block
#### Required elements
- request workflow evaluation CTA
- contact CTA
- optional demo-first CTA

#### Content requirements
- keep CTA flow soft and credible
- support both exploratory and direct inquiry paths
- avoid aggressive conversion language

## Recommended page CTA family
- Request a workflow evaluation
- Review a sample case
- See a documentation-ready summary
- Contact us

## Supporting asset requirements
- evaluator-focused page copy
- one evaluator-friendly case example
- one evaluator-friendly export example
- trust/evidence supporting language
- consistent Phase 1 CTA family

## Explicit exclusions
Do not include:
- ROI claims
- staffing-efficiency claims
- superiority claims
- institutional validation claims
- pricing complexity
- enterprise-sales language

## Page success criteria
The page is successful if:
- evaluators understand whether the product is relevant
- evaluators see what to review first
- the page supports demo-first trust-building
- contact/evaluation paths are clear but non-aggressive
- language remains clinician-readable and evidence-aware

## Implementation guardrails
- keep the page practical and concise
- emphasize interpretability, transparency, and workflow review
- prefer examples over claims
- align fully with validation/claims guardrails
