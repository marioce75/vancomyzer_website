# Vancomyzer FAQ Page Implementation Spec — Refined

## Purpose
Define the FAQ page as a tightly scoped, directly buildable Phase 1 page that reduces first-order confusion, reinforces trust, and supports exploration of the product.

## Refined page objective
A clinician visiting the FAQ page should be able to:
1. understand the most important workflow concepts
2. understand why transparency and interpretability matter
3. find concise answers to first-order questions
4. move from explanation to a useful next step such as a case, workflow page, or documentation example

## Final page structure

### Section 1 — Page intro
#### Required elements
- page headline
- short purpose statement
- optional CTA to workflow, sample case, or trust/evidence page

#### Content requirements
- frame the page as educational and practical
- keep tone clinician-readable
- support onboarding and trust-building

---

### Section 2 — Core workflow questions
#### Required question themes
- What is AUC-guided vancomycin dosing?
- How is Bayesian dosing different from traditional PK dosing?
- Why might AUC interpretation differ from trough-based reasoning?

#### Content requirements
- answers should be concise and educational
- focus on interpretation, not marketing
- avoid unnecessary jargon and long theory-heavy explanations

#### Priority
High priority. These are the most important first-order questions.

---

### Section 3 — Transparency and trust questions
#### Required question themes
- Why does transparency matter in a vancomycin dosing tool?
- What should clinicians review before accepting a recommendation?

#### Content requirements
- reinforce review behavior and clinician oversight
- connect naturally to the Trust & Evidence page
- avoid turning these answers into product claims

#### Priority
High priority. This section supports trust and risk reduction.

---

### Section 4 — Workflow and documentation questions
#### Required question themes
- Can documentation-ready summaries improve workflow?
- What kinds of cases are useful for learning vancomycin dosing?

#### Content requirements
- show practical value without making efficiency or ROI claims
- connect to sample case and export/example content
- keep answers concrete and short

#### Priority
Medium priority.

---

### Section 5 — CTA block
#### Required elements
- CTA to sample case
- CTA to workflow explanation
- CTA to documentation-ready example

#### Content requirements
- keep CTAs soft and educational
- move users toward exploration rather than hard conversion
- reinforce trust-building path

---

## Recommended FAQ answer style
- short answer first
- one practical explanation sentence or two
- optional link outward for deeper exploration
- avoid overly long answers on the page itself

## Recommended page CTAs
- Review a sample case
- See how the workflow is explained
- Review a documentation-ready summary
- Explore the Trust & Evidence page

## Supporting asset requirements
- final FAQ shortlist
- trust/evidence supporting copy
- one supporting sample case link
- one supporting export/example link
- one consistent CTA family

## Explicit exclusions
Do not include:
- long textbook-style explanations
- unsupported product-performance claims
- comparison-page style competitor language
- pricing/institutional messaging
- large resource-hub navigation blocks

## FAQ success criteria
The page is successful if:
- first-order confusion is reduced
- the page supports trust and onboarding
- answers remain concise and useful
- CTA paths are visible and practical
- language remains cautious and clinician-readable

## Implementation guardrails
- use the FAQ page to answer, not to sell
- keep answers short enough to scan
- prefer practical interpretation over abstract theory
- maintain consistency with trust/evidence guardrails
