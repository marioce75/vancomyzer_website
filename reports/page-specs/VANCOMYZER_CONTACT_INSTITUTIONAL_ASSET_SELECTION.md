## Reuse strategy
The Contact / Institutional page should reuse:
- trust/evidence supporting language
- the shared Phase 1 CTA family structure
- existing case/export assets rather than creating new evaluator-only variants

The Contact / Institutional page should intentionally differ by using:
- a more evaluator-oriented case than the homepage
- a richer export/example than the homepage quick summary

Rationale:
- preserves Phase 1 coherence
- makes the page more useful for serious evaluators
- avoids unnecessary asset sprawl

## Explicit non-selections
Do not use:
- homepage hero language as the main evaluator-facing summary
- CASE-001 as the primary institutional case
- quick summary export as the primary evaluator-facing export example
- pricing complexity
- ROI, productivity, staffing, or outcome claims
- enterprise-sales phrasing

## Implementation notes
- this page should feel more evaluator-oriented than the homepage
- examples should support review, trust, and documentation clarity
- contact should remain a soft endpoint after demo-first exploration
