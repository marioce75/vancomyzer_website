# Vancomyzer About Page Implementation Spec — Refined

## Purpose
Define a clear, practical About page that explains the mission, philosophy, and intended audience of Vancomyzer without drifting into marketing language or unsupported claims.

## Page objective
A clinician reading the About page should understand:

1. why Vancomyzer was created
2. what philosophy guides the design
3. who the product is intended for
4. how the product emphasizes transparency and interpretability
5. where to go next to explore the workflow or examples

## Final page structure

### Section 1 — Why Vancomyzer exists
Explain the problem space:

- complexity of vancomycin dosing interpretation
- limitations of opaque dosing tools
- importance of transparent workflow review

Content requirements:
- keep tone practical
- avoid dramatic language
- frame the product as supporting clinician interpretation

---

### Section 2 — Product philosophy
Key philosophy themes:

- transparency over opacity
- interpretability over automation
- clinician oversight over algorithmic authority
- documentation clarity for communication

Purpose:
- show the design intent behind the tool
- reinforce credibility and thoughtfulness

---

### Section 3 — Intended users
Define who the product is designed for:

- clinical pharmacists
- antimicrobial stewardship teams
- hospital clinicians reviewing dosing workflows
- learners exploring vancomycin dosing interpretation

Content requirements:
- keep this section simple
- avoid market-segmentation style marketing language

---

### Section 4 — How the tool supports review
Explain what the tool is intended to support:

- structured review of dosing workflow
- visibility of assumptions
- clearer interpretation of outputs
- documentation-ready communication

Source references:
- Trust & Evidence page
- sample case examples
- documentation/export examples

---

### Section 5 — Exploration CTA block
Recommended CTAs:

- Review a sample case
- Explore the workflow
- Review a documentation-ready summary
- Explore the Trust & Evidence page

Purpose:
- direct readers toward useful exploration paths
- maintain the demo-first philosophy

## Supporting asset requirements
The About page should reuse:

- homepage case example (CASE-001)
- homepage export/example preview
- trust/evidence messaging

Do not introduce new asset sets unless necessary.

## Explicit exclusions
Do not include:

- marketing slogans
- unsupported product claims
- competitor comparisons
- institutional sales language
- ROI claims

## Success criteria
The page is successful if:

- the mission and philosophy are understandable
- the page reinforces trust and transparency
- readers clearly understand who the tool is for
- readers can easily navigate to examples or workflow explanations

## Implementation guardrails
- keep tone professional and clinician-focused
- keep copy concrete rather than abstract
- maintain alignment with trust/evidence guardrails
- keep page concise and readable
