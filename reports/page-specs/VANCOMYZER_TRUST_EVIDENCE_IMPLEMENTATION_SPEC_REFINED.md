# Vancomyzer Trust & Evidence Page Implementation Spec — Refined

## Purpose
Define a build-ready Trust & Evidence page that reinforces credibility, transparency, and interpretability without implying unsupported validation.

## Page objective
After visiting this page, a clinician should:

1. understand how Vancomyzer emphasizes transparency
2. understand what assumptions and limitations they should review
3. see how workflow outputs are explained
4. understand how documentation-ready summaries may help communication
5. feel confident exploring a case or workflow example

## Final page structure

### Section 1 — Page introduction
Purpose:
Explain why transparency and interpretability matter in dosing tools.

Content requirements:
- emphasize review and interpretation
- avoid implying algorithmic authority
- explain the role of clinician oversight

---

### Section 2 — Transparency principles
Required highlights:

- visible assumptions
- visible calculation approach
- explicit limitations
- clinician-readable outputs

Purpose:
Show that Vancomyzer surfaces information that clinicians should review rather than hiding it.

---

### Section 3 — What clinicians should review
Explain key elements users should consider before accepting outputs:

- patient variables
- model assumptions
- data sufficiency
- interpretation context

Purpose:
Promote responsible interpretation rather than blind acceptance.

---

### Section 4 — Example workflow interpretation
Include:

- one sample case explanation
- short explanation of how results are interpreted

Source:
- reports/case-library/VANCOMYZER_CASE_LIBRARY.md

Purpose:
Demonstrate real workflow review.

---

### Section 5 — Documentation-ready communication
Include:

- example summary or export preview
- explanation of why documentation clarity matters

Source:
- reports/documentation/examples/INDEX.md

Purpose:
Highlight practical communication benefits without claiming efficiency gains.

---

### Section 6 — Evidence-aware communication
Explain:

- what the tool is designed to support
- what the tool does not claim

Source:
- reports/trust-evidence/VANCOMYZER_VALIDATION_AND_CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md

Purpose:
Make the claims guardrails visible and explicit.

---

### Section 7 — Exploration CTA
Recommended CTAs:

- Explore the workflow
- Review a sample case
- See how calculations are explained

Purpose:
Encourage deeper exploration without aggressive conversion language.

## Asset usage
Reuse existing assets whenever possible:

- primary trust-building case example
- primary documentation/export example
- claims guardrail language

## Explicit exclusions
Do not include:

- claims of validation
- claims of superior accuracy
- outcome improvement claims
- ROI claims
- institutional endorsement claims

## Page success criteria
The page is successful if:

- transparency and limitations are visible
- workflow interpretation is explained
- the page reinforces trust without marketing exaggeration
- it supports deeper exploration through cases or examples

## Implementation guardrails
- reuse homepage-selected assets where possible
- maintain cautious language
- prioritize clinician readability
- avoid dense technical jargon
