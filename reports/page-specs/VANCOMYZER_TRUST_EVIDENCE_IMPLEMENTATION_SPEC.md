Generated: 2026-03-12T23:53:19.855399Z

## Purpose
Define the trust and evidence page as a directly buildable Phase 1 page.

## Page objective
Show clinicians and evaluators how Vancomyzer approaches transparency, interpretability, caution, and evidence-aware communication.

## Section 1 — Page intro

### Required elements
- page headline
- short purpose statement
- optional CTA to sample case or workflow

### Content goals
- explain why trust and transparency matter
- keep the tone concrete and clinician-readable
- avoid sounding defensive or overly promotional

## Section 2 — What users should be able to review

### Required elements
- inputs used
- method/model used
- assumptions affecting output
- limitations and uncertainty
- caution/safety notes

### Content goals
- make review expectations explicit
- show what a responsible user should inspect
- reinforce interpretability over black-box trust

## Section 3 — Transparency principles

### Required elements
- visible assumptions
- visible rationale
- visible limitations
- documentation-ready communication support

### Content goals
- show the philosophy behind the product
- make transparency feel practical, not abstract

## Section 4 — Evidence-aware communication

### Required elements
- what the product is designed to support
- what the product should not imply
- caution around validation/superiority language

### Content goals
- prevent overstatement
- keep claims aligned with actual product behavior
- reinforce clinical credibility

## Section 5 — Practical examples

### Required elements
- one case example
- one export/documentation example
- CTA to review more examples

### Content goals
- make trust concrete through examples
- connect principles to real workflow

## Section 6 — Final CTA

### Required elements
- CTA to sample case, workflow, FAQ, or contact/evaluation

### Content goals
- direct the user to a trust-building next step
- keep conversion soft and education-first

## Recommended page CTAs

- Review a sample case
- See how the workflow is explained
- Review a documentation-ready summary
- Contact us for evaluation

## Required supporting assets

- trust/evidence copy
- one selected case example
- one selected export example
- claim guardrails
- FAQ/supporting trust items

## Messaging guardrails

- avoid unsupported validation claims
- avoid unsupported superiority claims
- keep language clinician-readable and concrete
- emphasize transparency, interpretability, and caution
