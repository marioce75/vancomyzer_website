## Key decisions
- Vancomyzer should not claim superiority, better outcomes, or validation beyond actual evidence.
- Messaging should emphasize transparency, interpretability, workflow support, and clinician-readable outputs.
- Comparison pages must remain educational and evidence-aware rather than adversarial or promotional.
- Any future claims about performance, validation, ROI, or clinical outcomes should require explicit supporting evidence before publication.
