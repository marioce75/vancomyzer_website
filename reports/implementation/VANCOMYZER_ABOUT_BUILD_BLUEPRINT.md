# Vancomyzer About Build Blueprint

## Purpose
Translate the refined About-page implementation spec into a direct build blueprint for Phase 1.

## Build objective
Create an About page that:
- explains why Vancomyzer exists
- clarifies the product philosophy
- identifies intended users
- reinforces transparency and interpretability
- sends visitors toward examples and trust-building paths

## Final page section order

### 1. Page introduction
Purpose:
- frame Vancomyzer in practical clinical terms
- establish mission without vague branding language

Assets:
- page headline
- short mission statement

Guardrails:
- avoid slogans
- avoid unsupported claims
- keep tone clinician-focused

---

### 2. Why Vancomyzer exists
Purpose:
- explain the problem space and why clearer workflow review matters

Themes:
- complexity of vancomycin dosing interpretation
- need for transparent workflow support
- need for clinician-readable outputs

Guardrails:
- keep this concrete
- avoid exaggerated problem framing

---

### 3. Product philosophy
Purpose:
- define the core design principles

Themes:
- transparency over opacity
- interpretability over black-box automation
- clinician oversight over algorithmic authority
- documentation clarity for communication

Cross-link:
- Explore the Trust & Evidence page

Guardrails:
- align language with trust/evidence guardrails
- avoid superiority claims

---

### 4. Intended users
Purpose:
- clarify who the tool is for

Selected audience themes:
- clinical pharmacists
- antimicrobial stewardship teams
- hospital clinicians reviewing dosing workflows
- learners exploring vancomycin dosing interpretation

Guardrails:
- keep the section simple
- avoid marketing segmentation language

---

### 5. How the tool supports review
Purpose:
- connect philosophy to practical product use

Assets:
- CASE-001 support link
- quick summary export support link
- Trust & Evidence support link

Guardrails:
- use links to examples rather than long duplication
- keep this section concise

---

### 6. Final CTA
Purpose:
- direct visitors toward useful next exploration steps

Allowed CTA options:
- Review a sample case
- Explore the workflow
- Review a documentation-ready summary
- Explore the Trust & Evidence page

Preferred emphasis:
- primary CTA: Review a sample case
- secondary CTA: Explore the Trust & Evidence page

Guardrails:
- keep CTA tone educational
- do not make contact the dominant CTA here

## Cross-linking rules
About must link to:
- sample case
- Trust & Evidence
- documentation-ready summary

Do not link primarily to:
- SEO pages
- comparison pages
- newsletters
- feature pages
- institutional-heavy flows

## Asset map
- mission/philosophy -> About-page source copy
- case support -> CASE-001
- export support -> quick summary export
- trust reinforcement -> Trust & Evidence page
- CTA family -> locked shared CTA family

## Implementation guardrails
- keep the page concise
- keep one support case only
- keep one support export example only
- keep tone practical and clinician-readable
- preserve Phase 1 scope

## Builder note
This blueprint should allow implementation of the About page without adding new sections, new asset variants, or broader brand-story content during Phase 1.
