# Vancomyzer Webinar and Educational Event Plan

Generated: 2026-03-12T23:43:04.839294Z

## Purpose
Define how Vancomyzer can use webinars and educational events to build trust, deepen engagement, and support clinician education.

## Primary goals
- turn educational content into live or semi-live engagement
- reinforce trust through useful clinician-facing teaching
- create deeper interaction than static pages alone
- support ongoing audience growth and retention

## Recommended event types

### 1. Short educational webinar
- focused topic such as Bayesian dosing, AUC workflow, or transparency in dosing tools
- practical and concise
- designed for trust-building, not hype

### 2. Case-based review session
- walk through one or two realistic vancomycin dosing cases
- explain assumptions, workflow, and caution points
- support both education and product understanding

### 3. Workflow demo session
- show how a user moves from inputs to outputs
- highlight documentation-ready summaries and transparent logic
- keep the tone educational

### 4. FAQ/live Q&A session
- answer common clinician questions
- clarify Bayesian/AUC misunderstandings
- connect attendees to deeper resource-hub content

## Suggested first webinar topics

- AUC-guided vancomycin dosing: practical interpretation
- Bayesian vancomycin dosing explained in clinician-readable terms
- Trough vs AUC: what actually changes in interpretation
- Why transparency matters in vancomycin dosing workflow
- Documentation-ready dosing summaries and clinical communication

## Event CTA ideas

- Register for the educational session
- Reserve a spot for the case review
- Get the recap and case pack
- Explore the related sample case

## Messaging guardrails

- keep events educational and credible
- avoid aggressive selling
- avoid unsupported claims
- align all event descriptions with real product capabilities

## Repurposing opportunities

- turn webinars into blog posts
- turn Q&A into FAQ content
- turn case reviews into case pages
- turn event takeaways into digest and social content
