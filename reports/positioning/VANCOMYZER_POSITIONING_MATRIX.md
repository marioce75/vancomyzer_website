## Initial hypotheses
- Vancomyzer should win on transparency and explainability.
- Vancomyzer should position itself as clinician-friendly rather than enterprise-heavy.
- Educational value should be treated as a product and marketing differentiator.
- Workflow clarity may be a stronger adoption lever than claiming superior precision alone.

## Messaging angles to test
1. Transparent Bayesian vancomycin dosing
2. Clinician-friendly AUC workflow
3. Clear calculations you can trust
4. Faster interpretation, not just more math
5. Precision with explainability

## Guardrails
- Do not claim superiority without evidence.
- Do not imply validated clinical advantage without proper support.
- Keep all marketing claims consistent with actual product behavior.
