# Spanish (Spain) terminology review — 11 October 2026

71 catalog entries revised or added; one tutorial subtitle cue clarified. English source keys, existing French/Portuguese catalog values, numeric templates, raw numbers, input parsers, units, model parameters and validation claims are preserved. New catalog keys retain the existing Portuguese override translations. The homepage model-input sentence is translated as a complete sentence rather than fragments around “Colin 2019”.

## Terminology and sources

- Use *perfusión*, *dosis de carga*, *dosis de mantenimiento*, *pauta posológica*, *ajuste*, *estimación bayesiana*, *concentración medida*, and *aclaramiento intercompartimental* in the appropriate contexts. [AEMPS product information](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/91206/FT_91206.html), [SEFH pharmacokinetic monitoring](https://www.sefh.es/bibliotecavirtual/fhtomo1/cap212.pdf).
- Preserve specimen specificity (serum versus plasma), estimated creatinine clearance versus estimated glomerular filtration, and model drug clearance. No equations or clinical thresholds imported. [Spanish nephrology consensus](https://revistanefrologia.com/en-documento-informacion-consenso-deteccion-manejo-articulo-S0211699521001612).
- AUC remains AUC; CMI is the Spanish MIC term. No AUC mg·h/L output is renamed AUC/CMI. The [SEFH/SEIP pediatric consensus](https://www.revistafarmaciahospitalaria.es/es-documento-nacional-consenso-monitorizacion-terapeutica-articulo-S1130634324000333) is a terminology reference only, not a source of adult treatment recommendations.
- Keep existing decimal notation and parser behavior. Both point and comma are accepted in Spain by [Centro Español de Metrología](https://www.cem.es/sites/default/files/2021-01/UtilizacionComaPunto.pdf).
- US nurse practitioner and physician assistant roles retain their English role names in parentheses; they are not represented as equivalent Spanish regulated professions. US regulatory statements retain their jurisdiction and lack-of-approval claims.

## Semantic review and limits

The level-entry component computes elapsed hours from **start of the most recent perfusion** to sample collection. The revised labels reflect that code; they do not reinterpret elapsed time as time since the end of perfusion. “Drug handling” labels pharmacokinetic parameters, so “Farmacocinética” replaces a literal handling translation. Modeled peak/trough labels explicitly retain “estimada”.

The entire existing catalog (3,214 original entries), numeric-template inventory and Spanish tutorial caption file were scanned for terminology, fallbacks and relevant field semantics. This is a targeted clinical-language review, not certification of every sentence. The prerecorded Spanish audio and embedded UI footage were not regenerated or comprehensively audited. The short caption “sangre y de perfusión” remains a narration-review item: changing its timing meaning without verifying audio would be inappropriate. The one edited cue clarifies “monitorización terapéutica”; all cue timestamps are unchanged. No changes were made to the separate dosys.health project.

The parent researcher's independent browser coverage was homepage-only. All responsive/workflow coverage in the companion report was performed locally on the Mac.
