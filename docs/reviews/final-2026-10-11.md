The final populated French 320px result uncovered a further page-width leak: absolutely positioned screen-reader labels escaped the static results-table scroller. Making that scroller a positioned containing block keeps the page at 320px without removing the accessible labels or the intended table scrolling.

Valid synthetic requests remain quantitatively identical to the known-good requests. No dosing equations, model parameters or scientific/validation claims were changed.

English model citations and report metadata remain source wording.
