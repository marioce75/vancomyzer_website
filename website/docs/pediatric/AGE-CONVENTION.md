# Postmenstrual-age convention of the pediatric preview

Date: 2026-10-29. Applies to `src/lib/pediatric/colin2019/prior.ts` (`postmenstrualAgeYears`, `clinicalPostmenstrualAgeWeeks`) and `src/lib/pediatric/engine.ts`. Independent reference values: section D of `docs/pediatric/reference-cases.json` (generated by `scripts/pediatric/reference-cases.py`, standard library only) and replayed by `src/lib/pediatric/__tests__/reference.test.ts`.

## 1. What the source model does

The model covariate is postmenstrual age (PMA) in **years**. The sources mix two year lengths:

| Source statement | Where | Implied convention |
|---|---|---|
| `PMA_R = 35+(40/52)` — the reference individual used to normalise maturation and decline | MOESM2 control stream | 1 year = 52 weeks for the 40-week gestation term |
| "Postmenstrual age for patients other than neonates was assumed to be 40 weeks longer than the recorded postnatal age (years)" | article Methods 2.3 | PMA = postnatal years + 40 weeks |
| "PMA in years was derived from the datasets or assumed to be PNA plus gestational age with a default of 40 wk for subjects with missing gestational age" | Table 1 footnote a | PMA = PNA + GA (units as recorded by each study) |
| `IF(mPNA.LT.0) mPNA = (PMA-40/52)*365` and `IF(PNA.GT.0) GA = (PMA*365 - PNA)/7` | MOESM2 control stream (derived variables) | 1 year = 365 days when converting between PMA, PNA (days) and GA (weeks) |
| MAT50 printed as 46.4 weeks for e^(−0.113) = 0.8931 years | Table 3 | 1 year = 52 weeks for the week/year conversion |

The preview previously computed PMA = (GA + PNA_days/7)/52 for everybody, i.e. a 364-day year for the postnatal term. That reproduced neither the reference individual (a 35-year-old came out at 35.89 instead of 35.77 years) nor the article's non-neonatal rule (6 years postnatal gave 6.790 instead of 6.769 years). The clearance effect was negligible (< 0.05 % above 2 years, < 0.2 % in neonates) but the convention was wrong and undocumented.

## 2. Convention now used everywhere

```
PMA (years) = postnatal age (chronological years, 365.25 days) + gestational age at birth (weeks) / 52
```

Postnatal age is normalised to days first: days as entered; weeks × 7; months × 365.25/12; years × 365.25. Gestational age is required below 1 year of postnatal age and otherwise defaults to 40 weeks (stated as an assumption in every result, as the authors did).

Properties, each verified in the reference cases:

- A 35-year-old born at 40 weeks has PMA = 35 + 40/52 = PMA_R exactly, so the normalisation reference is reproduced.
- Any non-neonate with GA 40 has PMA = postnatal years + 40/52, the article's rule, exactly (6 y → 6.7692; 72 months gives the same value).
- For neonates the clinical convention GA + PNA/7 weeks, converted at 52 weeks/year, differs by PNA × (1/364 − 1/365.25) years: at most **0.33 days** over the dataset's neonatal postnatal range (1–95 days).
- The control-stream identity (GA×7 + PNA)/365 differs by at most **0.85 days** in the neonatal range and **3.7 days** at 17.999 years.
- Clearance sensitivity to one day of PMA (reference cases): 0.74 % at the 23.4-week floor, 0.73 % at 30 weeks, 0.50 % at 40 weeks, 0.004 % at 6 years, ≈0 at 17 years. The author-variant deviations therefore change clearance by at most ≈0.6 % (neonates) and ≈0.01 % (children).

Both values are displayed: the **model PMA** in years (and × 52 as "model weeks") and the **clinical PMA** in weeks (GA + completed days/7). Every result's assumptions list states the formula and the numbers used.

## 3. Boundaries evaluated on the model PMA

| Boundary | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest supported PMA | model PMA × 52 ≥ 23.4 weeks (0.45 y) | Table 1, Lo et al. minimum PMA 0.45 y |
| Dataset gap | refuse if 0.88 < PMA < 2.58 y (open interval) | Table 1; see DATASET-COVERAGE.md |
| Adult boundary | refuse if postnatal age ≥ 18.0 chronological years | preview scope (adult calculator exists) |

For a term birth the gap corresponds to postnatal days 40.5–661.4; for a birth at 24 weeks, 152.8–773.8 days. The refusal message computes the postnatal-day equivalents for the gestational age actually entered.

## 4. Reference cases (section D)

Nineteen cases from the reference individual through the PMA floor (GA 22 + 10 d accepted at 23.42 weeks; GA 22 + 1 d refused), the gap edges (term 40 d accepted / 41 d refused; 661 d refused / 662 d accepted; GA 24 + 150 d accepted / 160 d refused), 12 and 18 months (refused), 2, 6 and 17.999 years (accepted) and 18.0 years (refused). The Python script computes the expected status from the published boundaries independently of the engine; the TypeScript test asserts the status, the field named in the refusal, the model and clinical PMA to 1e-12 and, where accepted, all four typical parameters to 1e-9.
