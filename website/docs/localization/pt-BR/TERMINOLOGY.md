# Brazilian Portuguese terminology for review

Scope: terminology only. No Brazilian protocol doses, targets, eligibility rules, sampling schedules, or renal-function assumptions were imported into either calculator. These references do not constitute endorsement, partnership, regulatory approval, or independent validation of Dōsys or Vancomyzer.

Primary terminology references consulted:

- [HC-UFMG/Ebserh — monitorização dos níveis séricos](https://www.gov.br/hubrasil/pt-br/hospitais-universitarios/regiao-sudeste/hc-ufmg/saude/protocolos-assistenciais-hc-ufmg-ebserh/PR_308_Monitorizacao_niveis_sericos_voriconazol_vancomicina_aminoglicosideos_V01.pdf). Used to check Brazilian clinical language for monitoring, serum levels, collection, and institutional protocols.
- [HU-UFGD/Ebserh — protocolo farmacoterapêutico de vancocinemia](https://www.gov.br/hubrasil/pt-br/hospitais-universitarios/regiao-centro-oeste/hu-ufgd/acesso-a-informacao/pops-protocolos-e-processos/gad/prt-ufcli-002-protocolo-farmacoterapeutico-de-vancocinemia-em-pacientes-pediatricos-e-neonatais.pdf). Used for terminology such as loading/maintenance doses and infusion. Its pediatric/neonatal scope was NOT imported into the adult software.

Preserve AUC₂₄ versus interval AUC, steady-state versus historical exposure, measured concentrations versus model predictions, and unindexed CrCl versus indexed kidney-function estimates. A modeled peak is not a new blood-sampling instruction. A credible band excluding measurement error is not a prediction interval for a new blood result. Developer synthetic checks are not independent clinical validation. Pending validation must not be described as completed or underway.

The existing English FDA/US-law language is retained in meaning, not converted into an ANVISA claim. US professional titles, products, units, dollar amounts and contracts do not establish Brazilian availability, licensure, support, reimbursement or regulatory status. No Portuguese-speaking support service was promised.

Bilingual Brazilian clinical and legal review is still required. The glossary is a draft editorial choice, not clinical sign-off.
